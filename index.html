About Us

Bridging the Digital Divide in Jind

Ravinder CSC & Typing Center operates under the Common Service Centre (CSC) scheme, a cornerstone of the Digital India initiative. Located on Old Court Road, Courier Market, Jind, we serve as a reliable one-stop office for property registry, legal documentation, and CSC services, ensuring paperwork is accurate, affordable, and handled with care.

From Online Registry (Sale, Transfer, Lease, Mortgage & Adoption Deeds) to DTP NOC, Stamp Paper, TDS on property sale, Marriage Files, Name Change Gazette, Affidavits and all CSC-related work — our experienced team handles every document with accuracy and transparency, saving you the trouble of running between offices.
